# RA 12009 — New Government Procurement Act (NGPA) & its IRR: Full Analysis

Grounded in the **full text of the law** (117 sections) and the **complete official IRR**
(GPPB Resolution 02‑2025 of 04 Feb 2025, published 10 Feb 2025, effective 25 Feb 2025 —
193‑page GPPB PDF; Lawphil's web copy is truncated at IRR §54).

> **Bottom line for aipms:** RA 9184 is **repealed**. The NGPA swaps *lowest‑bid default*
> for **fit‑for‑purpose & proportionality with four award criteria, makes **PhilGEPS
> mandatory** (e‑bidding, e‑payment, open data), adds a **beneficial‑ownership & relation
> disclosure** regime, hardens anti‑fraud/eligibility, professionalizes practitioners (PRC),
> and imposes a **3‑year transition**. These are rule-bundle changes on one engine — exactly
> the "mode over fork" model aipms already assumes.

## 2. Governance principles (Art. I §2–3)

Eight principles now decide policy & interpretation:
1. **Transparency** — open‑contracting style disclosure at all stages.
2. **Competitiveness** – equal opportunity for eligible private/public suppliers.
3. **Efficiency** – organized, uniform process using **emerging tech & innovation**.
4. **Proportionality (new, emphasized)** – requirements/method/contracts must be proportional to need & contract nature. **Fit‑for‑purpose** approach runs through the whole IRR.
5. **Accountability** – officials investigated/held accountable.
6. **Participatory procurement / public monitoring**.
7. **Sustainability** – whole‑life value for money, minimal environmental impact.
8. **Professionalism** – qualified standards, competency & certification, **code of ethics**.

> Interpretation rule: in case of doubt, resolved **in favor of government** (IRR §2).

---

## 3. Scope & exclusions (IRR §4)

**Covered:** Goods, Infrastructure (civil works), Consulting Services, regardless of funding source (local/foreign). Lease of goods & real property included.

**Excluded from the Act/IRR:**
- ODA grants under RA 8182/8555 (unless agreed otherwise).
- Real property acquisition → RA 10752 (Right‑of‑Way).
- PPP → RA 11966 (PPP Code), except GoP‑financed portions.

**Not procurement activities:**
- Direct cash/in‑kind assistance to beneficiaries.
- Scholarships, trainings, conferences, seminars, strategic planning.
- COS / Job Order engagements.
- Disposal of government property (unless specified in bid docs).

## 4. Strategic procurement planning (Art. II / Rule II §7–19)

- **APP / PPMP chain:** End‑User units compile PPMPs → BAC Secretariat consolidates into an **Indicative APP** → BAC recommends mode → **final APP approved by HoPE**, posted on the website, submitted to GPPB by end‑January. **No procurement without an approved (Indicative or final) APP** (§7.8).
- **Multi‑Year Contracting (MYCA)** from DBM for multi‑year projects; ABC = MYCA value (§9).
- **Early Procurement** (EPA) — allowed after Indicative‑APP approval and NEP/budget submission, *short of award*, pending funding (§12).
- **Design‑Build** scheme for Infrastructure (§14) — one contractor for design + construction.
- **Procurement Agent/Agency** (§15) — another expert government agency runs the BAC functions; cannot issue the Notice of Award; service fee arrangements.
- **Framework Agreement** (§16) — option-type contract, single or multi‑supplier, later guideline‑driven.
- **Pooled Procurement** (§17) — lead procuring entity + collaborating entities, demand aggregation.
- **Renewal of regular/recurring services** (§18) — 1‑year renewals (extendable for office space rental, telecoms); requires performance evaluation.
- **Market scoping** (§10), **lifecycle assessment/cost analysis (LCA/LCCA)** (§13) — mandatory where applicable, green criteria.
- **Warehousing & inventory** (§19) — cloud inventory via DICT consultation.

**Subcontracting** (§7.5): ≤ **20%** of Goods / ≤ **50%** of Infrastructure of ABC value; general contractor remains liable; subcontractors must meet the eligibility criteria.

---

## 5. Procurement by electronic means (Art. III / Rule 20) — **mandatory, not optional**

**PhilGEPS** is the **single electronic procurement portal** — primary source of info & channel for ALL procurements, *"covering procurement planning until payment."* Procuring Entities **shall register and fully use** it. Features:

- Electronic bulletin board (posting of ITB/RFEI);
- Registry of suppliers with **Platinum Certificate** (annual validity);
- Electronic catalogue + **Virtual store** (CSE purchase);
- **E‑marketplace** (search/compare/buy from qualified merchants);
- **E‑bidding** (from planning to payment);
- **E‑small‑value** procurement;
- **E‑reverse‑auction**;
- **E‑payment / electronic transfer (BSP‑supervised channels);
- **Open data** platform (public monitoring).

- **Systems interconnectivity** (§23): GPPB may mandate cross‑database sharing (PhilGEPS→GoP‑OMR, DTI registrations, CIAP/CPES/PCAB, LGU permits, **BIR tax**, **SEC/CDA beneficial ownership**, Insurance‑Commission sureties, Procure Entities' project status), *notwithstanding* the **Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)** — with trade‑secret protection.
- **Electronic signatures** must comply with the **E‑Commerce Act (RA 8792)**.

## 6. Modes of procurement (Art. IV §26 / Rule 26)

**11 modes** (Fit‑for‑Purpose choice; GPPB may adapt with tech):
1. **Competitive Bidding**
2. **Limited Source Bidding** (specialized / national security)
3. **Competitive Dialogue** (complex/innovative; two‑stage)
4. **Unsolicited Offer with Bid Matching** (new concept; invite comparative)
5. **Direct Contracting** (proprietary, patents, exclusive dealer)
6. **Direct Acquisition** (ABC ≤ **₱200,000**)
7. **Repeat Order** (≤25% of quantity, within 6 months of NTP, same/lower unit price)
8. **Small Value Procurement (SVP)** (ABC ≤ **₱2,000,000**; requires ≥3 quotes, 1 sufficient)
9. **Negotiated Procurement** (only in the IRR‑listed causes)
10. **Direct Sales** (non‑CSE previously awarded by another PE, 6‑mo window)
11. **Direct Procurement for STI** (R&D supplies, startups, innovation)

**SVP LGU thresholds** (IRR §34.2): Barangays ₱100K; Municipality 5th/4th‑class ₱200K, 1st–3rd class ₱400K; Cities & Provinces from ₱800K (5th) to ₱2M (1st/2nd). Municipality only ≤₱400K. (Full tiered table in IRR §34.2.)

**Negotiated procurement** (§35): Two failed biddings; Emergency; Take‑over; Adjacent/Contiguous; Agency‑to‑Agency; Scientific/artistic/exclusive‑work; Highly technical consultants; Defense agreements; Lease of real property/venue; NGO participation; Community participation (farmers/fisherfolk, *community/social groups*); UN/international agencies; Direct retail of POL/EV‑charging/online subscriptions (≤ **₱1,000,000** ABC standard, up to ₱5M with HoPE approval).

**CSE** (§40): only from **PS‑DBM**, subject to availability; fallback sources when stocks unavailable/insufficient/not meeting specs.

**Anti‑splitting** (§39): cannot split to evade thresholds.

## 7. BAC & oversight (Art. V / Rule 41)

**BAC:** 5 or 7 members, chaired by ≥3rd‑ranking permanent official; rotating appointments **annual**, max **3 consecutive terms**; **Chief Accountant cannot be regular member**; HoPE cannot be a member; observers (see below). **Quorum** = majority incl. Chair or Vice‑Chair; BAC performs eligibility, evaluation, post‑qualification, recommendation to HoPE, procurement monitoring reports.

**Mandatory video recording & livestreaming** of procurement conferences for Competitive Bidding above:
- Goods > **₱10,000,000**
- Infra > **₱20,000,000**
- Consulting > **₱5,000,000**

Recording kept ≥ **5 years**; national‑security procurements decided by HoPE (thresholds adjustable by GPPB).

**Observers:** COA rep + **≥2 observers** (one private-sector/professional body, one CSO/PO) at eligibility check, short‑listing, pre‑bid, preliminary examination, evaluation, post‑qualification; no voting; submit reports within 7 days (silence presumes procedure was followed).

**Honoraria**: up to 30% of annual/3rd‑quarter salary for BAC/TWG/Secretariat.

**Professionalization (§46):** competency & certification framework, code of ethics; DBM creates procurement positions; **PRC** establishes licensing + continuing education as a condition for holding procurement positions.

## 8. The bidding process (Rules VI–X)

**Bidding documents**: include ABC, ITB, Instructions, eligibility (SLCC/NFCC, etc.), specs (green), bid/price forms, securities, contract conditions. Brand names prohibited unless compatibility/interoperability exceptions.

**Publication**: continuous posting on the Procuring Entity website / socials (or newspaper fallback) and on **PhilGEPS** — 7 calendar days for competitive modes.

**Pre‑procurement conference** (mandatory except small): resolves whether award is based on **LCRB / MEARB / MARB / HRRB**.

**Pre‑bid conference** (§51): mandatory for ABC **≥ ₱3M** competitive; held ≥12 days before receipt (open & consistent). **Bid bulletins** ≤7 days before receipt deadline.

**Eligibility (Goods §52.4)**:
- Philippine citizen/sole proprietorships; partnerships/corporations with **≥60% national** own; cooperatives; JVs (≥60% Filipino; significant exceptions for infra 25–60%).
- **SLCC**: similar work ≥**50% of the ABC**, within **10 years** (or combination: ≥1 similar ≥25% + aggregate ≥25%).
- **NFCC** = [(Current Assets − Current Liabilities) × 15] − ongoing/uncompleted commitments; must be **≥ ABC**; alternatively a committed **line of credit ≥ 10% ABC** (foreign bank → confirmed by a local bank).
- **PhilGEPS Platinum Certificate** mandatory; supporting documents validated at post‑qualification. Forgery/misrepresentation → automatic disqualification + suspension.

**Two‑envelope bid (separate sealed tech & financial):** simultaneous submission; bids late → rejected. **Anti‑splitting**; **equal access** to info; no disclosure before official release.

**Bid Security (§56):** guaranteed ≤10 days from NOA + furnish performance security.
- Cash/cashier's/manager's check: **2% ABC**
- Bank draft/guarantee/irrevocable LC: **5%**
- Surety bond (IC‑accred, callable‑on‑demand): **5%**
- Bid Securing Declaration (BSD) allowed for listed sectors.
→ PE must offer **at least 3 forms** incl. BSD; securities **denominated in PHP**; returned only after contract signing + performance security.

**Bid validity (§57):** **≤ 120 days** from bid opening; nothing forces extension.

**Bid opening (§58):** immediately after deadline; for the LCRB both envelopes open the same day; for MEARB/MARB, financials remain sealed until qualifying technical scores known. Abstract + minutes available on request/fee.

**Preliminary analysis of bids (§59):** non‑discretionary **"pass/fail"** on technical documents; any missing → fail. Then financials opened for passing bids. **ABC is the ceiling**; bids over ABC are auto‑disqualified; no floor (§60).

**Evaluation — Goods/Infra (§61):**
- **LCRB / LCB**: rank ascending; lowest. ≤7 days.
- **MEARB / MEAB**: quality‑price ratio; quality assessed on ~12 soft/hard criteria (fitness, track record, design, approach, green, staff, delivery, disposal…). **Price weight = 15–40%**; the rest to quality. ≤ **14 days**.
- **MARB** (£) most‑advantageous‑on‑quality (top technical first, then financial opened).
- **Other evaluation periods**: 3–20 days for consultancy.
- **Bid evaluation** no communication with bidders until NOA; clarifications in writing.

**Consultant evaluation (§62):** Short‑list 3–7; **QBE** (quality only, financial for negotiation) or **QCBE** (quality‑cost, price weight 15–40%); **HRRB award**. Evaluation ≤ **21 days**. Negotiations ≤10 days.

## 9. Post‑qualification & award (Rules X–XI)

- **Post‑qualification (§63):** lowest/HR/ME/Bid with 5 days submits PhilGEPS Platinum back‑up + ICP/BOQ + ICO/tax (latest filed, preceding quarter ≥ promised) etc.; BAC verifies legal/technical/financial on **non‑discretionary** criteria ≤ (post‑qual per PE of 12 days). Success → LCRB/MEARB/MARB/HRR; award at lower of bid vs calculated.
- Failures cascade to next rank.

- **Failure of bidding (§64):** no bids, all ineligible, no responsive bid, or winner refuses; re‑publish/rebuild; after 2nd failure → negotiated procurement.

- **Award (§66):** HoPE act within **10 days** of BAC recommendation; issue **Notice of Award (NOA)**; winning bidder executes contract & posts performance security within **10 days** of NOA; NTP within **3 days**. Period from bid opening to award ≤ **60 days** (ext. to 90).

- **Performance Security (§68):**
  - Cash/cashier's/manager's check: Goods & Consulting **5%**; Infra **10%** of contract price.
  - Bank draft/LOC / surety bond (callable): **30%**.
  - Valid until Certificate of Final Acceptance; update before variation order; ≤50% proportional reduction only on >10% reductions; Infra: additional for >10% cumulative increases; BSD allowed for listed sectors.

- **Failure to enter contract / to post PS → forfeit bid security + sanctions (§69).**

- **Reservation Clause (§70):** reject any & all bids, declare failure, or not‑to‑award (collusion/evidence, procedural failure, fundamental change of conditions).

## 10. Price, warranty & contract implementation (Art. 11–12/18)

- **Fixed prices** (§89); no escalation except extraordinary >10% on specific infra components (PBSA regional indices formulas by GPPB).
- **Warranty (§90):**
  - **Goods**: minimum **3 months** (Expendable) / **1 year** (Non‑expendable); retention **1–5%** of every progress payment OR special bank guarantee 1–5% of contract (default **1%**).
  - **Infra:** full‑responsibility from commencement until final acceptance; defects liability (DLP) **1 year** covered by the performance security, repairs within **90 days**; structural‑defect/failure warranty: **15 yrs** permanent (type 4/5 buildings, steel/concrete structures per NBCP/DPWH defaults), **5 yrs** sem‑per (housing types 1/2/3, concrete/asphalt roads, lined channels, etc.), **2 yrs** other (Bailey bridges, shallow wells). **Milestone/retention money 10%** of progress payments.

---

## 11. Sustainable & inclusive procurement (Art. XII / Rule 12)

- **Sustainable/green PP**: whole‑life value; **Green PP strategy** phased (DTI‑led green product certification).
- **Inclusive**: micro/social/startups/communities; set‑aside for **registered sectors** (farmers, fisherfolk, ‑₱, solo parents, MSME/social enterprises, startups, cooperatives).
- **Domestic preference (§79):** shift to a **domestic bidder if its bid is NOT more than 25% higher than lowest foreign bid** (with waivable conditions). Lifecycle/green weight in award.

## 12. Disclosure & anti‑corruption (Art. 15/16/20–21)

- **Disclosure of relations (§81):** sworn affidavit that bidder (or any officer/board/partner) is **not related within the 3rd civil degree** to HoPE/BAC/TWG/Secretariat/End‑User/PMO/consultants; failure to disclose → **automatic disqualification**.
- **Beneficial‑ownership disclosure (§82):** bidders must disclose ultimate owners; **GPPB‑maintained public online registry**; false entries → blacklisting (see §100 below).
- **Anti‑splitting** (§39); **audit trail**; reserved anti‑collusion (data analytics reports annually; quarterly procurement reports to GPPB incl. green/MSME/women‑run contracts).

**Sanctions (Art. 20–21):**
- Public officers → administratively liable (opening/divulging bids, delays, undue influence, splitting, simulating eligibility, non‑compliance).
- Private entities:
  - **Suspension 1 yr** (1st) / **2 yrs** (2nd) for: refusal to post PS, bid withdrawal, failure to furnish docs, poor performance → negative slippage ≥15%, top‑title violation etc.
  - **Blacklisting 1 yr / 2 yrs** for: collusion/cartelized bid‑rigging (≥3 groundsf; fraud/falsified docs; beneficial‑ownership false; infringement of confidentiality; multiple‑bid shams; forced/coerced turnover; etc.), plus other infra/proc.
  - **Perpetual disqualification** for: 3rd blacklist offense (own+other PEs aggregate posted on GPPB portal), or infra‑warranty‑repair non‑compliance within 90 days.
  - Sanctions extend to **controlling entities, beneficial owners, spouses (for sole prop), partners, shareholders ≥20% + third‑degree relatives, chair/president, allied entities, JV members** (§105) — a powerful share‑of‑responsibility rule.
- **Bid/performance‑securing declaration enforcement**; immediate suspension at the blacklisting proceeding commencement; posting to GPPB portal within 7 days.

**Protest & disputes (Art. 16–17):**
- Earlier: **request for reconsideration** (3 days from disapproval; resolve 7 days, one only). 
- **Claim protest fee schedule** (electronic):

| ABC | Fee |
|---|---|
| ≤ ₱50 M | 0.75% ABC |
| >50‑100 M | ₱500,000 |
| >100‑500 M | 0.5% ABC |
| >500 M‑1 B | ₱2,500,000 |
| >1 B‑2 B | 0.25% of ABC (floor ₱M) |
| >2 B | ₱5,000,000 |

- HoPE resolves protest within **7 days**.
- **Finality**: decision final & executory for **Goods ≤₱20M, Infra ≤₱50M, Consulting ≤₱10M** (LGU lower: 1.25M / 12.5M / 2.5M). Above & NOA → appeal to the RTC under Rule 65 of the Rules of Court within 10 days.
- **Non‑interruption**: prevailing protest does not stay the procurement; resolved fully before award.
**No TRO / preliminary injunction** against procurement except by the **Supreme Court** (§87).

## 15. What changed vs RA 9184 (impact summary)

1. **Philosophy**: from "lowest‑calculated responsive bid" default → **4 award criteria** (LCRB, MEARB, MARB, HRRB) with **fit‑for‑purpose & proportionality**; domestic preference set to **25%** margin; green/sustainable criteria weighted into evaluation.
2. **E‑procurement is compulsory**: PhilGEPS end‑to‑end (planning→payment) — **e‑bidding, e‑payment, e‑reverse‑auction, e‑marketplace**; the Open Data platform.
3. **New transparency/anti‑fraud**: mandatory video recording; beneficial‑ownership **public registry**; automatic disqualification for undisclosed relations; interconnection of BIR/SEC/LGU/etc. databases notwithstanding the Data Privacy Act; electronic protest filing.
4. **New sectors & inclusion**: registered‑sector set‑asides, micro/social/startups, green market, community procurement.
5. **New bidding mechanics**: streamlined eligibility, two-envelope opening, post-qualification validation of the PhilGEPS Platinum Certificate; professionalization (competency & certification).
6. **Sanctions widened**: sanctions (including the wide content extension) are enforceable against beneficial owners/relatives/‑controlling entities; protest‑fee schedule; electronic protest filing; perpetual disqualification.
7. **Procurement reform**: professional licensing via PRC; the Procurement Agent; dispute‑arbitration per ADR Act; required video recording; **jury‑duty** rig; disclosure; procurement data analytics.

**For aipms:** NO hard‑coded "lowest bid" logic — encode the **four award criteria**, model **beneficial‑ownership/relation disclosures**, treat **PhilGEPS e‑channels as a required adapter** (not data), and make the **NGPA rule‑bundles interchangeable with enterprise/private runs** during the 3‑year transition (per‑instance config, not a fork).
